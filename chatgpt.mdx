ChatGPT can use BrowserPair to carry out tasks in the Chrome profile you already use. ChatGPT decides what to do; BrowserPair keeps it inside the limits you choose and executes each step in its Chrome extension.

<Note>
  The [published BrowserPair plugin](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ac5859a310c8191b959b4cfd69b2668) is the recommended way to connect ChatGPT. You do not need to create a custom MCP server or enable ChatGPT Developer mode for this setup. Availability may depend on your ChatGPT plan and workspace.
</Note>

## Add BrowserPair

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your browser">
    Pair the Chrome profile you want ChatGPT to use. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the published ChatGPT plugin">
    Open [BrowserPair in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ac5859a310c8191b959b4cfd69b2668) directly. This is the official plugin, not a manually configured MCP server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with OAuth">
    Select **Connect** in ChatGPT and sign in with the BrowserPair account that owns your Chrome profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it in BrowserPair">
    ChatGPT sends you to BrowserPair. Check that the identity is published by **chatgpt.com**, choose the intended browser and confirm the requesting app, then select **Allow**. Ask-before settings are account-wide under Permissions. See [Connect an AI app](/connect-ai-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional: custom MCP connection

If your ChatGPT workspace supports custom MCP connections and you intentionally choose that route instead of the published plugin, use `https://mcp.browserpair.com` with OAuth. That separate route may require ChatGPT Developer mode. ChatGPT Developer mode is not the same as BrowserPair's Developer permission mode. See [Remote MCP](/remote-mcp).

## Try it

- "Which of my browsers are connected to BrowserPair?"
- "Open example.com in my browser and tell me the page heading."
- "Look up Ada Lovelace on Wikipedia in my browser and give me her birth year."

When a step needs your approval, ChatGPT tells you to approve or deny it in the BrowserPair card in Chrome. Ask ChatGPT to stop a task at any time; work already done on websites is not undone.

## Limits

ChatGPT uses the browser you connected. There is no per-app website editor; tasks and account Permissions govern website changes alongside Chrome policy. Standard mode asks about sensitive steps by default. Developer mode adds no operation confirmations; required sign-in/file steps and immutable task confirmations remain protected. BrowserPair can request masked screenshot observations; displaying image content depends on the ChatGPT connection and tool response support. Each browser-accepted task counts toward your monthly BrowserPair task allowance.

## Disconnect

Remove BrowserPair in ChatGPT, or select **Disconnect** under **AI apps → Connected apps** in the [BrowserPair App](https://app.browserpair.com/). Either way ChatGPT loses access immediately.
